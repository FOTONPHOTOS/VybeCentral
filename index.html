Our Story

Born in Lagos.
Built for Africa.

What began as a duplex became a movement. The ground floor is home — the upstairs is yours. Three premium spaces crafted for musicians, DJs, and creators who live and breathe Afro culture.

From Afrobeats to Amapiano, Afro-soul to highlife — if it has African rhythm, this is your studio. We are rooted in Nigeria but our doors are open to every corner of the continent and diaspora.
